# Codeforces Master Sheet (Beginner to Intermediate)

This tracking sheet is designed for developers aiming to grow from an absolute beginner up to a solid **Intermediate (1500-1600 rating)** level on Codeforces. It focuses heavily on mastering Div. 2 A, B, and C problems.

> **Difficulty Scale:** 
> ⭐ = 800–1000 (Div 2. A - Fundamentals)
> ⭐⭐ = 1100–1300 (Div 2. B - Thinking & Logic)
> ⭐⭐⭐ = 1400–1600 (Div 2. C - Algorithms & DS)
> ⭐⭐⭐⭐ = 1700–1800 (Stretch Goals)
>

## 💡 Codeforces Advice for 800 - 1500 Rating

1.  **Don't overcomplicate Div 2. A and B**: They almost *never* require advanced algorithms (like Segment Trees or Graph Flows). They require logic, greedy choices, or simple math. If your solution for A/B is 100 lines long, you are overthinking it.
2.  **Learn the C++ STL**: You MUST be comfortable with `std::vector`, `std::map`, `std::set`, `std::sort`, `std::lower_bound`, and `std::priority_queue`.
3.  **Mind the Overflows**: Use `long long` (`ll`) for answers that can exceed $2 \times 10^9$.
4.  **Use your Templates**: Make sure to use the `Codeforces_CPP_Templates.md` boilerplate for fast I/O.
5.  **Stop doing Random Problems**: Grind tags methodically. If you are weak at Binary Search, do 10 Binary Search problems in a row until the pattern clicks.
