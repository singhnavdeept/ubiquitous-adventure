**UNIT 1 — Ethics & IT**

**Ethics** — A set of moral principles that guide a person's behavior. Ethics = deciding right vs wrong.

**Integrity** — Doing the right thing even when no one is watching. Consistency between values and actions.

**Business Ethics** — Applying ethical principles in business decisions. Covers fairness, honesty, responsibility.

**Code of Ethics** — A formal document stating ethical standards employees must follow.

**Ethical Work Environment** — A workplace where ethical behavior is encouraged through policies, leadership, and training.

**Whistleblower** — Someone who reports unethical/illegal activity inside an organization.

**Ethical Decision Making** — A step-by-step process: identify issue → gather facts → evaluate options → decide → act → reflect.

**IT Ethics** — Rules governing behavior of IT professionals regarding privacy, data, and online conduct.

**IT Professional Ethics** — Protect user privacy, avoid piracy, no malware creation, maintain confidentiality.

**Common Ethical Issues for IT Users** — Piracy, hacking, plagiarism, cyberbullying, data theft, unauthorized access.

**MCQ traps Unit 1:**
- Ethics ≠ Law (legal doesn't always mean ethical)
- Integrity = doing right when unobserved
- Whistleblower = reporter, not a hacker
- IT professional's #1 duty = protect user data

**UNIT 3 — Startups & Government Schemes**

**Startup** — A new tech-driven business aiming to solve a problem and scale rapidly.

**Startup India** — Govt of India initiative launched **January 2016** to promote innovation and startups.

**Startup India Benefits** — 3-year tax exemption, self-certification, fast-track patent, seed funding, easier winding up in 90 days.

**Eligibility** — Age ≤ 10 years, turnover ≤ ₹100 crore, recognized by DPIIT.

**NASSCOM 10,000 Startups** — NASSCOM initiative to incubate and support 10,000 tech startups. Provides mentorship, cloud credits, networking.

**STP (Software Technology Park)** — IT-only govt scheme. Benefits: 100% export-oriented, duty-free imports, tax holidays.

**SEZ (Special Economic Zone)** — Broader zones (IT + manufacturing) with tax exemptions and relaxed customs. Not IT-only.

**STPI** — Software Technology Parks of India. It is an organization (not the scheme itself) that registers STP units.

**SBIR** — US federal program funding R&D in small businesses. No university tie-up needed. 3 phases: feasibility, development, commercialization.

**STTR** — Similar to SBIR but **requires collaboration with a university/research institution**.

**NSF Grants** — National Science Foundation. Funds fundamental early-stage research.

**MCQ traps Unit 3:**
- Startup India = Jan 2016 (not 2014/2015)
- SBIR = no university needed; STTR = university required
- SEZ ≠ STP (SEZ is broader)
- STPI is an organization; STP is the scheme
- DPIIT recognizes startups, not NASSCOM

**UNIT 4 — IT Startup Business Plan**

**Executive Summary** — Written LAST, placed FIRST. Brief overview of the entire plan. Purpose: convince reader to invest/read more.

**General Company Description** — Company's mission, vision, legal structure (Pvt Ltd, LLP), and uniqueness.

**Products and Services** — What you sell, your USP, how it solves a problem.

**Marketing Plan** — Target market, competitor analysis, pricing, promotion, distribution.

**Operational Plan** — Day-to-day running: location, equipment, supply chain, production process.

**Management and Organization** — Founders, org chart, roles, advisory board. Shows investors the team can execute.

**Personal Financial Statement** — Founders' personal assets and liabilities. Required for loan applications.

**Startup Expenses and Capitalization** — All costs to launch: equipment, licenses, salaries. Determines capital needed.

**Financial Plan** — Projected income statement, balance sheet, cash flow, break-even analysis. Most important for investors.

**Appendices** — Optional supporting docs: patents, contracts, research data, resumes.

**Refining the Plan** — Business plan is a living document, updated based on feedback and market changes.

**MCQ traps Unit 4:**
- Executive summary = written last, placed first
- Financial Plan ≠ Startup Expenses (financial plan has projections)
- Marketing Plan = about the customer, not the product features
- Operational Plan = HOW the business runs
- Appendices = optional, not mandatory
- Business plan is NOT a one-time document
